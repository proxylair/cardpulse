Bandai dropped six new One Piece Card Game starter decks on July 31, 2026 --
Monkey.D.Luffy (Red), Roronoa Zoro (Green), Kuzan (Blue), Charlotte Katakuri
(Purple), Sabo (Red/Black), and Eustass "Captain" Kid (Yellow). All six
launched at the same MSRP, but they are not trading at the same price two
weeks later -- and the gap tells you something useful about which decks
actually have chase-worthy cards inside them.

Current market prices for the sealed decks:

| Starter Deck | Leader | Current Price |
|---|---|---|
| ST-32 | Roronoa Zoro (Green) | $30.47 |
| ST-34 | Charlotte Katakuri (Purple) | $23.35 |
| ST-33 | Kuzan (Blue) | $20.41 |
| ST-36 | Eustass "Captain" Kid (Yellow) | $19.73 |
| ST-35 | Sabo (Red/Black) | $17.19 |

**Why ST-32 (Zoro) is pulling ahead:** it's not the leader card driving the
premium -- it's what's riding along with it. The Zoro deck's individual
notable cards are already outpricing the others on their own: Kouzuki Oden
is sitting at $7.43 and Dracule Mihawk at $4.95, which together are worth
more than a third of the whole deck's sealed price. That's the sign of a
deck where the support cards, not just the leader, have real demand.

By comparison, ST-33 (Kuzan) and ST-35 (Sabo) are trading closer to "leader
card plus a small premium for the rest of the deck" -- their sealed price
roughly tracks what you'd expect from just the leader alone, which usually
means the supporting cards in those decks haven't found a home in
competitive lists yet.

**The practical takeaway:** if you're buying to open and build, ST-32 and
ST-34 currently have the best odds of the deck being worth more than what
you paid, card-for-card. If you're buying purely for the leader to slot into
an existing deck, ST-35 and ST-33 are the cheaper way to get there.

*Prices are live sealed-product market snapshots as of the article date and
will move as more of each deck gets opened -- worth rechecking before buying
multiple copies.*
