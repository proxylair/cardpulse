If you're building a Standard deck on a budget, the cards worth watching aren't
the bulk commons -- they're the small group of uncommons, rares, and mythics
that sit in the $2-$8 range. That's usually where a card is good enough to see
real play, but not yet expensive enough to price players out.

Below is a live snapshot (pulled today via TCGCSV, a free mirror of
TCGplayer's product/price feeds) of the highest-priced Standard-legal cards
currently under $8. A few of these are worth a second look if you're
trading, buying singles, or deciding what to pick up before a price jump.

**A quick read on what stands out:**

Duskmourn: House of Horror and Wilds of Eldraine each place two cards in the
top ten, which tracks -- both sets are still deep into their Standard rotation
window and their strongest mythics haven't fallen off yet. Kaito, Bane of
Nightmares in particular is worth watching: planeswalkers that hold this kind
of price outside the "chase" tier ($15+) often do so because they're a
consistent 2-3 of in multiple competitive lists -- though that's a read on
the data, not a confirmed reason, and speculative hype can't be ruled out.

The two Marvel's Spider-Man cards are a good example of a crossover set
holding value better than most people expected at release -- worth watching
whether that holds or fades as the set ages further from its print run.
