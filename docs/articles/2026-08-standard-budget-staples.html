10 Standard-Legal MTG Cards Under $8 Worth Knowing (August 2026)

Magic: The Gathering 2026-08-18

If you're building a Standard deck on a budget, the cards worth watching aren't the bulk commons -- they're the small group of uncommons, rares, and mythics that sit in the $2-$8 range. That's usually where a card is good enough to see real play, but not yet expensive enough to price players out.

Below is a live snapshot (pulled today via the Scryfall pricing API) of the highest-priced Standard-legal cards currently under $8. A few of these are worth a second look if you're trading, buying singles, or deciding what to pick up before a price jump.

A quick read on what stands out:

Duskmourn: House of Horror and Wilds of Eldraine each place two cards in the top ten, which tracks -- both sets are still deep into their Standard rotation window and their strongest mythics haven't fallen off yet. Kaito, Bane of Nightmares in particular is worth watching: planeswalkers that hold this kind of price outside the "chase" tier ($15+) usually do because they're a consistent 2-3 of in multiple competitive lists, not because of speculative hype.

The two Marvel's Spider-Man cards are a good example of a crossover set holding value better than most people expected at release -- worth watching whether that holds or fades as the set ages further from its print run.
